Run bundle adjustment jointly refines camera poses and 3D points by minimizing the pixel distances between fixed observations and projected points. The simulated adjustment slider lets you explore a separate interpolation toward known ground truth. Moving either slider clears the solver result. Camera position and structure RMSE use the original world coordinates and depend on the reconstruction’s global alignment.
