Light field explorer

Drag the main view to move the virtual camera (u, v). Click anywhere in the scene to choose an image coordinate (s, t). A light field L(u, v, s, t) is a 4D function — every combination of viewpoint and pixel is one sample.

Camera array (u, v plane) — 25 captured input views arranged by camera position. Click any thumbnail to jump to that viewpoint. Purple frames = four views used to synthesise the current virtual camera position.

Virtual output view — synthesised by blending the four nearest input cameras. The orange crosshair marks the selected output pixel (s, t). Foreground objects shift more than background as you drag.

Ray space diagram — the orange point is your selected pixel on the image plane (s, t). Blue marks show which camera positions (u, v) contribute rays through that pixel. Foreground has higher disparity.

This scene is synthetic so that foreground, middle-ground, and background have clearly different parallax. Notice how the tree (foreground) moves much more than the house (mid) or hills (far) as you drag the camera — this disparity is the signature of depth in a light field.
